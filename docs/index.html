MORROR ART
The app · Android

Sets up your speaker in a few minutes.

WiFi for the lyrics, a sync check, and the look of the glass. No account, no login. The app updates itself afterwards.

Download the app
morror-art-app.apk · about 0.4 MB · Android 8 and up

Google will warn you. Here is what to tap.

This app is not on the Play Store yet, so your phone treats it as unknown. That is expected. Four taps.

  1. The download warning

    Chrome asks before saving any app that is not from the Play Store.

    “morror-art-app.apk might be harmful. Do you want to download it anyway?”

    Tap Download anyway.

  2. Allow installs from Chrome

    Open the download. The first time, the phone says it is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source.

    Tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.

  3. Play Protect

    Play Protect says it does not recognise the developer. If it offers to scan the app, let it: the scan passes.

    “Unsafe app blocked” or “App scan recommended”

    Tap More details, then Install anyway.

  4. Open it

    Tap Open. Setup walks you through Bluetooth, WiFi, your preferences and a sync check with a song you know.

After this, no more warnings. Updates arrive inside the app: one tap on the Speaker tab. You never repeat these steps.

Without the app

The speaker works on its own. Pair over Bluetooth and play. For lyrics, press the dial, choose WiFi, pick your network and type the password with the dial: turn to choose a letter, press to type it, press done.

MORROR ART · the speaker keeps every setting on the glass; the app is a remote for it.
