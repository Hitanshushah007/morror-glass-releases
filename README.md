# MORROR ART — set up your speaker with the app

**Before you start:** plug the speaker in and switch it on. Have your home WiFi password ready.

**1. Install the app** (Android)
Open this link on your phone and tap the file it downloads:
https://github.com/Hitanshushah007/morror-glass-releases/releases/latest/download/morror-art-app.apk
If your phone asks, allow installs from Chrome once, then tap **Install**. Open **MORROR ART**.

**2. Pair the speaker over Bluetooth**
- In the app, on the **Setup** screen, tap **Bluetooth settings**. Allow the Bluetooth permission if asked.
- In your phone's Bluetooth list, tap **MORROR ART** to pair. (Not showing? Press the speaker's power key once and look again.)
- Go back to the app and tap **I've paired it**. Step 1 should say **Paired · MORROR ART**.

**3. Put the speaker on your WiFi**
- Tap **Scan for networks**. The speaker looks around and lists the WiFi networks it can see.
- Tap your home network, type the password, tap **Join**.
- Watch the status: **Joining…** then **Connected**. (Wrong password? It tells you, and you can try again. Office or hotel networks with a login page do not work.)

**4. Let the app find the speaker**
- Make sure your phone is on the **same WiFi** you just chose.
- Step 3 shows **Speaker online** with its address. If it does not, tap **Find on this WiFi**.

**5. Play music**
Play anything from any music app on your phone over Bluetooth. Lyrics appear on the glass by themselves.
The **Look** tab changes colours and lyric styles; **Images** puts your own photos behind the lyrics.

**Moving house or changing WiFi?** Open the app, Setup screen, and repeat step 3.
**Something stuck?** Switch the speaker off and on, then open the app again.
